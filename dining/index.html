Collection 03

Dining furniture

Modern dining tables, seating and storage — the pieces that turn a dining room, kitchen or open-plan space into a place to gather.

The Collection

Dining sets that come together easily

Our dining collection is built to mix. Tables and seating share a modern design language, so retailers can offer matched sets or let customers build their own combinations.

From compact tables for city apartments to larger pieces for family dining rooms, the range covers the sizes New York households need.

  • Matched sets or mix-and-match pieces
  • Sizes for apartments and full dining rooms
  • Modern finishes and upholstery
What We Carry

Inside the dining collection

Tables

Dining tables

Rectangular and round dining tables in modern finishes for everyday and open-plan rooms.

Chairs

Dining chairs

Upholstered and side dining chairs that pair with our tables or sell on their own.

Stools

Counter & bar stools

Counter- and bar-height stools for kitchen islands and breakfast bars.

Storage

Sideboards & buffets

Buffets and sideboards that add dining-room storage and serving space.

See It In Person

View dining sets at our Brooklyn showroom

Finishes and proportions are easier to judge in person. Trade customers are welcome to visit our 57th Street showroom in Sunset Park, Brooklyn, to see current dining stock before placing an order.

Open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM.
