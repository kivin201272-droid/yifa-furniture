About Yifa

A New York furniture importer with two decades of experience

Yifa Furniture — operating as Yifa Furniture Inc. — has been one of the importers and distributors of modern furniture in the United States since 2002. From our base in Sunset Park, Brooklyn, we supply furniture businesses with contemporary collections at trade pricing.

Our focus is straightforward: dependable products, clear policies and reliable delivery across the New York metro area.

  • Direct importing keeps trade pricing competitive
  • Showroom and warehouse under one roof
  • Delivery throughout Brooklyn, Queens, Manhattan and beyond
